Insights — tap questions about your own memories

A private, tap-first guide to what you've already saved. Ask about upcoming dates, a person, a memory, a gift, or a plan — and get an instant answer pulled straight from your own local data.

  • No cloud AI. No model download, no server call — Insights reads only your local database, on your device.
  • Never invents anything. Answers come only from what you've actually saved. Nothing is generated or guessed.
  • Tap to open. When an answer mentions a person, memory, or plan, tap it to jump straight there.

Separate from Insights: Create a Card — a freeform card maker for any photo on your phone, with 16 styles, captions, and sizing for feeds and posts.

Your data never leaves you

Privacy is not a feature.
It's the foundation.

KeepMemo was built offline-first on purpose. Your memories stay on your device unless you decide otherwise.

Completely free & ad-free

No ads, no subscriptions, no paywalls. Built as the opposite of apps that monetize your attention.

No account needed

Open the app and start. No email, no sign-up, no tracking profile created behind the scenes.

Everything stays local

Data lives in a local database on your phone. Even Insights answers using only that local data — no cloud AI, ever.

Optional, and only if you choose: export a backup file anytime, enable periodic local auto-backup, or sign in with Google to sync backups to your own Drive. Nothing leaves your device without your action.

        Common questions
        
Frequently asked questions about KeepMemo

        
Quick answers about this private journal, birthday reminder, and offline memory app.

      

      

        
Is KeepMemo free?
Yes — completely free. No paid tier, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases required to use the app. Download it on Google Play and start your private journal with no account.

        
Does KeepMemo have ads?
No. KeepMemo is ad-free. There are no banners, no video ads, and no "watch an ad to unlock" features. The app was built to stay free for everyone without selling your attention.

        
Does KeepMemo work offline?
Yes. It is an offline-first journal app. Birthdays, memories, gifts, plans, and notes all work without internet. Backup and Google Drive sync are optional and off by default.

        
Is my data private?
Yes. Your private notebook stays on your device by default. There is no account sign-up and no cloud AI reading your journal. You can also enable app lock with PIN or biometrics.

        
What is Insights?
Insights is a tap-first way to ask questions about what you've already saved — upcoming dates, people, memories, gifts, and plans. It runs entirely on your device using your local data, with no cloud AI and no language model download.

        
Can it remind me of birthdays and anniversaries?
Yes. KeepMemo is a birthday reminder and anniversary reminder app. You get gentle notifications the day before and on the day, with flexible frequencies.

        
What is On This Day?
On This Day resurfaces a memory from the same date in a past year — like finding an old entry in your emotional diary. It helps you rediscover moments you saved.

        
Can I track gifts and make memory cards?
Yes. The gift tracker logs gifts given and received by person. Create a Card turns any photo into a beautiful memory card you can save or share.
