ask, don't dig —

Insights

A tap-first way to ask about what you've already saved. No typing required — just tap a question.

  • No cloud AI. Runs entirely on your device, using only your own local data.
  • Never invents anything. Every answer comes from something you actually saved.
  • Tap to open. Mentions a person or memory? Tap it to jump straight there.

Separately, Create a Card turns any photo into a shareable card — 16 styles, captions, sizing for feeds and posts.

      common questions —
      
Frequently asked

    

    

      
Is KeepMemo free?
Yes — completely free. No paid tier, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases. No account required.

      
Does KeepMemo have ads?
No. No banners, no video ads, no "watch to unlock." The app stays free without selling your attention.

      
Does it work offline?
Yes, fully offline-first. Backup and Google Drive sync are optional and off by default.

      
Is my data private?
Yes. Everything stays on your device by default — no account, no cloud AI, optional PIN/biometric lock.

      
What is Insights?
A tap-first way to ask questions about your saved data. Runs entirely on-device, no cloud AI, never invents answers.

      
What is On This Day?
A memory from the same date in a past year, resurfaced each morning — like finding an old letter.

      
Can I track gifts and make cards?
Yes — the gift log tracks gifts by person and occasion, and Create a Card turns any photo into a shareable design.
